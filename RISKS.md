# Risks & Disclosures

Department of Rug Efficiency ($RUG) is an experimental blockchain project.

## Token contract

The token contract is designed without:

- additional minting
- transfer taxes
- blacklist controls
- pause controls
- administrator keys
- trading restrictions

The `rugPull()` function is a parody function that always reverts.

## Important limitations

The slogan:

> The only rug that cannot rug.

is part of the project's parody branding.

It does not mean that ownership of RUG is risk-free.

Token holders may still face:

- market volatility
- liquidity risk
- smart-contract risk
- blockchain/network risk
- third-party application risk
- loss of funds
- unofficial liquidity pools
- fraudulent tokens impersonating RUG

## Permissionless markets

RUG is a standard permissionless ERC-20 token.

Third parties may create liquidity pools or markets without approval from the project.

Only pools explicitly identified through official D.O.R.E. project channels should be considered project-associated pools.

## Testnet

The current deployment is on Arc Testnet.

Testnet tokens have no monetary value.
